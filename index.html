Choco Merge: Merge It – Privacy Policy

Developer: Cagverse · Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Choco Merge: Merge It ("the game") handles, why, and the choices you have. The game does not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email address, or other contact details.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, Merge Points, diamonds, cosmetics, quests, statistics) and settings (sound, vibration, ad preference) are stored on your device. We do not upload this data to our own servers. Uninstalling the game deletes it, unless you turned on the optional cloud save described below.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and the ad networks it works with. To deliver and measure ads, these partners may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device information (model, operating system, language, network type),
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks, rewards earned).

On first launch the game asks whether ads may be personalized. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized based on your advertising ID. Declining also tells our ad partners not to sell or share your personal information for targeted advertising (your "Do Not Sell or Share" choice under California law). You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Ad Preferences. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Gameplay analytics

If you accept on the first-launch privacy screen, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Unity Gaming Services (Unity Analytics) so we can find levels that are too hard and improve the game. This includes level starts, wins and losses, moves and time spent, power-ups used, ads watched, offers shown, and purchases, together with a random installation ID and basic device information (model, operating system, language, country). It does not include your name, email address, or advertising ID. If you decline, no gameplay statistics are collected. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Ad Preferences.

The game also uses Unity Remote Config to receive game settings (for example reward amounts and ad frequency). This uses the same anonymous installation ID and does not collect personal information.

Cloud save and recovery code

Cloud save is optional. If you choose Settings → Save Progress → Back Up Now, the game creates an account with Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication and Cloud Save) identified only by a randomly generated recovery code. Your game progress (level, Merge Points, diamonds, power-ups, purchases owned, cosmetics, statistics and settings) is then stored online so you can restore it on another phone by entering the code. No name, email address or phone number is needed. If you never back up, no progress is sent online.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your cloud save at any time in the game: Settings → Save Progress → Delete Cloud Save. This permanently deletes your online account and the progress stored with it; progress on your phone stays. If you no longer have the game installed, email us at cagverse@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your recovery code, and we will delete your account and data within 30 days. Anonymous gameplay statistics can be deleted as described under "Gameplay analytics".

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminder notifications on your device (for example when a daily challenge is waiting). They are created locally on your phone; no push service or personal data is used. You can turn them off at any time in your Android notification settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases (diamonds, Merge Points, No Ads, Starter Pack, Piggy Bank) are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We receive only a purchase receipt, which the game checks on your device to deliver the item. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine, which may collect limited technical data (such as device model and crash information) to keep the engine working reliably, as described in Unity's privacy policy above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, UK, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data on our own servers, requests about advertising data are best directed to Unity and the relevant ad partners. To have your anonymous gameplay statistics deleted, choose Decline in Settings → Ad Preferences: the game then stops collecting them and asks Unity to delete the statistics already collected from your installation. We are happy to help. Contact us at the address below.

Türkiye (KVKK): If you are in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) applies. Cagverse is the data controller for the limited processing described in this policy. Advertising data and anonymous gameplay statistics are processed by Unity and its ad partners, which may transfer it outside Türkiye, based on your consent given in the game. Under Article 11 of KVKK you may ask whether your data is processed, request information, correction or deletion, object to processing, and withdraw your consent at any time (Settings → Ad Preferences). Send requests to the contact address below; we will respond within 30 days.

Security and retention

Data kept on your device stays there until you uninstall the game. A cloud save is kept until you delete it (see "Deleting your account and data"). Third-party partners keep data according to their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
