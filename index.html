Choco Merge: Merge It – Privacy Policy

Developer: Cagverse · Effective date: September 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Choco Merge: Merge It ("the game") handles, why, and the choices you have. The game does not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email address, or other contact details.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, Merge Points, diamonds, cosmetics, quests, statistics) and settings (sound, vibration, ad preference) are stored only on your device. We do not upload this data to our own servers. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and the ad networks it works with. To deliver and measure ads, these partners may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device information (model, operating system, language, network type),
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks, rewards earned).

On first launch the game asks whether ads may be personalized. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized based on your advertising ID. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Ad Preferences. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

In-app purchases

Purchases (diamonds, Merge Points, No Ads) are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We receive only a purchase receipt, which the game checks on your device to deliver the item. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine, which may collect limited technical data (such as device model and crash information) to keep the engine working reliably, as described in Unity's privacy policy above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, UK, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data on our own servers, requests about advertising data are best directed to Unity and the relevant ad partners. We are happy to help. Contact us at the address below.

Security and retention

Data kept on your device stays there until you uninstall the game. Third-party partners keep data according to their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
